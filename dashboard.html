FILE 006 // BRIEFING

Before you request access.

Is the KSH fee refundable?+

Each level is a one-time payment for lifetime access to that level's drawers. Refund requests are handled case-by-case — reach out within 48 hours of payment.

Do you give investment tips?+

No. Money Sense teaches how to think about risk, saving, and opportunity — it never tells you what to buy, bet, or invest in.

How do Live Sessions work?+

Sessions run on Google Meet on a published schedule. Pro and Elite members get the calendar invite automatically after joining.

Can I start my own Circle?+

Yes, from Pro level up. A Circle gets its own private space to plan, post updates, and track progress together.
